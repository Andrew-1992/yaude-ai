# Sanadi AI brand palette

- Black — #111111
- Cream — #F5F1E8
- White — #FFFFFF

No accent color. Functional UI states (links, errors, success, active) are
communicated through font weight, underline, and border rather than hue —
a deliberate choice to keep the system disciplined and distinct from the
blue/purple accents most AI products default to.

## Usage

- Dark surfaces (hero sections, cover slides): black background, cream
  headline text, muted cream-tinted secondary text (~70% lightness).
- Light surfaces (body content, cards): white or cream background, black
  text, grey (#6B6B6B or similar) for secondary/muted text.
- Cards on a black background: white or cream fill, black text (inverted).
- Cards on a white/cream background: black fill, white/cream text — this is
  the closest thing to an "accent" in the system, used sparingly for
  numbered steps, primary CTAs, and table headers.
